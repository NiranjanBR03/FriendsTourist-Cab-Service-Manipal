Our story

Travel with a local you can genuinely trust

Friends Tourist is run by Shiva Shetty and his son Atharv. Based in Manipal, the family-run team serves university students, visiting parents, medical travelers and tourists across Udupi and Coastal Karnataka. Fares and inclusions are confirmed before the trip, with no surprise charges later.
